# 🤖 AI Quest: Train Your Robot Friend!

**An Interactive AI Learning Game for Kids (Ages 8–12)**

Welcome, young explorers! 🌍 Join Byte the friendly robot on an epic adventure to discover the magic of Artificial Intelligence. Through fun games and challenges, you'll learn how AI really works — no coding experience needed!

## ✨ What Makes This Special?
- **Kid-Friendly Design**: Bright colors, cute characters, big buttons, and exciting animations
- **Hands-On Learning**: Actually "train" an AI and see it get smarter in real-time
- **3 Mini-Games** teaching core AI concepts:
  1. **Pattern Detective** – Learn pattern recognition (like how AI spots differences)
  2. **Label & Learn** – Supervised learning: Teach by example, watch AI classify new things!
  3. **Adventure Trainer** – Reinforcement learning: Guide your robot and reward good choices
- **Instant Feedback & Explanations**: After every activity, discover "How AI works" in simple terms
- **Progress Tracking**: Earn AI Stars ⭐ and unlock fun facts

## 🎮 How to Play
1. Open the `index.html` file in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox work best)
2. Meet Byte and start your AI training journey!
3. Complete all three activities to become an official AI Trainer
4. Share your high scores with friends and family

**No downloads or sign-ups required** — it's all in your browser!
